**Change:** re-applied the stateful windowed loader (from 1f00dfb) and added a per-slot carried-hidden-state norm clip (HIDDEN_CLIP_NORM=5, `h_state *= (5/(slot_norm+1e-6)).clamp(max=1.0)` after detach) — the standard stateful-RNN stabilization, hypothesizing the run-1f00dfb loss spike was hidden-state drift under stale-weight skew.

My earlier "no yielded window is all-pad" reasoning was wrong because it checked x, not y (x=chunk[:-1], y=chunk[1:] differ by the boundary shift). This is a dumb data bug, not an idea failure -> fix and re-run (sanctioned by program.md's "easy to fix, fix and re-run"). See a9d3795.
