**Source:** agent

**Result:** val_bpb 1.447489 (vs. 2.087648 baseline, −31% relative), top1 0.6762, top5 0.9317, memory 1.3GB, 1928 steps, 99.2M tokens, 0.959M params
**Notes:** Big, expected win — LSTM's gated cell state directly addresses the vanishing-gradient problem the assignment's long-range bar-line/meter structure needs. However `tokens_M` (99.2M) is only 24% of the run-1 median (407.0M), well under the `keep-prov` 60% threshold: at hidden_size=256 the LSTM's 4 gates cost ~4x an RNN's per-step recurrent compute (1928 vs 7898 steps, ratio 4.1x, matches theory), so part of this win could be "less overfitting from less data exposure" rather than a pure capacity/memory win. Marking `keep-prov` per program.md's mechanism; next experiment re-tests at a smaller `HIDDEN_SIZE` to roughly restore per-run token throughput and confirm the win holds at comparable exposure, before adopting the rest of `baseline-improve`'s transferable stack (windowed loader, learned (h0,c0), lr schedule, weight_decay, embed_size=256) and moving to the worktree's real target — long-sequence training.

## 5fc6b30 — discard
**Source:** agent
**Change:** Bundled 5 changes at once (adopting `baseline-improve`'s full transferable stack in one jump, per program.md's "you do NOT need to rediscover any of this" guidance): windowed truncated-BPTT loader (`TRAIN_SEQ_LEN=128`, `WINDOW_BATCH_SIZE=256`, ported from `baseline-improve`'s cf3c7b9), learned `(h0, c0)` initial state, `WEIGHT_DECAY` 0.0→0.05, `LEARNING_RATE` 0.003→0.0015 with 20-step warmup + time-based cosine decay, `EMBED_SIZE` 128→256. `HIDDEN_SIZE` held at 128 (run e980c7a's confirmed value).
**Result:** val_bpb 1.578275 (worse than e980c7a's 1.441281), top1 0.6557, top5 0.9173, memory 0.7GB, 8290 steps, 271.3M tokens (67% of the run-1 median — above the 60% `keep-prov` threshold, so this is a real regression, not a throughput-exposure confound), 0.368M params
**Notes:** Research citations for context (both actually fetched, not from memory): [Karpathy — The Unreasonable Effectiveness of RNNs](https://karpathy.github.io/2015/05/21/rnn-effectiveness/) and general truncated-BPTT literature note character-level BPTT windows of ~100 or less are traditional; a search also surfaced [sushant097/Music-Generation-Char-RNN](https://github.com/sushant097/Music-Generation-Char-RNN) (Irish-ABC char-LSTM) using `seq_len=64` with a **stateful** LSTM (state carried across windows) — worth re-testing stateful carry per program.md's own suggestion, separately from this bundle.
Surprise worth chasing: training loss (on the windowed batches) plateaus early — by step ~3200 (37% of the time budget, LR schedule still around 0.001-0.0015, not yet decayed to near-zero) it flattens around 1.05-1.09 nats and barely moves for the remaining 63% of the run. That rules out "LR decayed too fast" as the main story and points instead at the combination of the windowed loader (model learns local 128-token patterns fast) with `weight_decay=0.05`/`embed_size=256` not translating into a generalization win on the full-sequence `val_bpb` harness. With 5 variables changed at once this can't be isolated — reverting the whole bundle (`git reset --hard` to e980c7a-branch tip 1156e1d) rather than cherry-picking, and re-testing the windowed loader change alone (holding `LEARNING_RATE=0.003`, `WEIGHT_DECAY=0.0` at run-3's confirmed values) as the next, more isolated experiment.
